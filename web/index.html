How to read this

  • Lines are published history; dashed lines are the forecast; the shaded band is the range the model gives 80% odds to.
  • Outlook score: house prices 25%, interest rates 20%, growth & investment outlook 20%, construction growth 15%, fenestration imports 20%. Missing signals are skipped. Above +15 = Expanding, below −15 = Contracting.
  • Imports cover aluminium structures (HS 7610), PVC doors & windows (392520), building hardware (830241) and insulating glass (7008). For big producers such as China, imports are a weaker signal than for import-reliant markets.
  • Sources: BIS (policy rates, house prices, exchange rates), IMF World Economic Outlook, World Bank, UN Comtrade, FRED commodity prices. All free and public.
  • This is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Forecasts extend past patterns and cannot foresee policy shocks.
